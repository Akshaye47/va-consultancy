Questions

Common visa planning questions.

Quick answers before you book a detailed consultation.

Which Australian visa should I apply for?

The right visa depends on your occupation, qualifications, English level, relationship status, sponsor options, study goals and previous visa history.

Can you help after a refusal?

Yes. A refusal needs careful review of the decision record, deadlines and evidence gaps before choosing a review, fresh application or alternative pathway.

Do I need a registered migration agent?

In Australia, immigration assistance is generally provided by registered migration agents or Australian legal practitioners. Add your real registration details here before launch.

What documents should I prepare first?

Start with identity documents, visa history, qualifications, employment records, English results and relationship or sponsorship evidence where relevant.
