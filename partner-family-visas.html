Partner & family

Build a clear evidence story for your family visa.

Relationship evidence

What we review

Partner and family applications rely heavily on consistent evidence, eligibility of the sponsor, timing, identity documents and relationship history.

Common pathways

  • Partner visa subclass 820/801
  • Partner visa subclass 309/100
  • Prospective Marriage visa subclass 300
  • Parent, child and other family options

Key documents

  • Relationship timeline
  • Joint finances and household evidence
  • Photos, travel and communication records
  • Sponsor documents
  • Identity and character documents

How VA Consultants helps

We organise relationship evidence into a clear structure, identify weak areas, and help you prepare statements and supporting material that are consistent and easy to review.

Best for

Married couples, de facto partners, fiance cases and families needing a careful document strategy.
