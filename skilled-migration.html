Skilled migration

Check your pathway for skilled migration to Australia.

For professionals whose occupation, qualifications and experience may support a points-tested visa pathway.

Eligibility focus

What we review

Skilled migration usually depends on your nominated occupation, points score, skills assessment, English level, age, work history and state nomination options.

Common pathways

  • Skilled Independent visa subclass 189
  • Skilled Nominated visa subclass 190
  • Skilled Work Regional visa subclass 491
  • Temporary Graduate visa subclass 485 planning

Key documents

  • Passport and visa history
  • Qualifications and transcripts
  • Employment references and payslips
  • English test results
  • Skills assessment evidence

How VA Consultants helps

We map your likely points, identify the best occupation strategy, review skills assessment readiness, and explain whether state or regional nomination may be realistic for your profile.

Best for

Engineers, IT professionals, healthcare workers, trades, accountants, teachers and other occupation-listed applicants.
