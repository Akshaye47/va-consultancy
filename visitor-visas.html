Visitor visas

Prepare a stronger visit plan for Australia.

For tourism, family visits, short business activities and temporary stays.

Temporary stay evidence

What we review

Visitor visa preparation focuses on purpose of visit, financial capacity, travel history, ties to home country and reasons to return after the stay.

Common pathways

  • Visitor visa subclass 600
  • Tourist stream
  • Sponsored family stream
  • Business visitor stream

Key documents

  • Invitation letter or itinerary
  • Bank statements and employment evidence
  • Family or property ties
  • Previous travel history
  • Purpose of visit documents

How VA Consultants helps

We check the story behind the visit, identify refusal risks, and help organise supporting documents so your temporary stay purpose is clear.

Best for

Tourists, parents visiting family, short business visitors and applicants with previous travel concerns.
